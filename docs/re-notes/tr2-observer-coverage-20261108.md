## Cause and bounded strategy

`src/13-exports.wat:$run` takes a block budget; `src/04-cache.wat` chains blocks while that budget remains. One block can contain many instructions. Folds can also retire multiple blocks on `run(1)`, as explicitly reported by `get_last_run_blocks`. Internal WAT callbacks/reentry remain inside the call and are not exposed as JS observation boundaries. The existing JS adapter cannot claim instruction coverage or other-thread coverage.

The repaired adapter observes contiguous ordinary `run(1)` returns until its existing **2500 ms installation deadline**, explicit removal, failure, or first complete poll/Action pair. It does not replace 4096 with a larger lifetime count. Its work is bounded by the original caller's remaining signed-i32 block budget, with a deadline check at each observed boundary, and at most **32 retained records**. It subtracts actual retired blocks, including fold overshoot, rather than the old one-per-call debit. A zero-progress result removes the observer and forwards the unspent ordinary budget once. Zero/negative budgets and entry yield servicing are forwarded rather than suppressed. After removal/expiry it forwards the remaining ordinary budget and preserves aggregate retired-block reporting. No additional guest budget, guest setter, debug flag, translator/materialization, callback invocation or private guest write is introduced.

Installation still prearms the Worker expiry timer. The ordinary helper now supplies a one-use activation callback to its existing passive sample, **after native keyDown**, with the independent native release timer already armed. Before activation, ordinary calls pass through unsplit and consume no observation allowance. Activation does not reset or extend the installation deadline. The first complete paired poll/Action removes the facade immediately with status `witnessed`; this bounds work and avoids spending the record cap on repeated polls after the needed witness. A failed HRESULT, zero Control bytes or clear Action is still a raw observed outcome, not a fabricated input defect or firing success. Final correlation still requires a physical VK17-down sample wholly within the unchanged native interval and an in-interval poll/Action pair. Out-of-interval, unarmed or missing-physical results fail qualification without retry.

Owner, thread, endpoint, EXE and loaded-code checks, frozen direct-window reads, call-stack pairing, RPC-publication validation and Action successor checks remain. Expiry, record overflow, bad code/stack/read, replacement, trap and incomplete pairing fail closed. A stalled WAT/host call cannot run a Worker timer until it returns; the observer cannot preempt that call. Independent native keyUp and the enclosing runtime's external lifecycle deadline remain necessary. No Worker timer or observer response controls native hold duration.
